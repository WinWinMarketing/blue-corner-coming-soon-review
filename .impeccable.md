## Design Context

### Users

Blue Corner serves Canadian men who may be carrying burnout, isolation, anxiety, addiction, or crisis without naming it, plus therapists and founding partners who want to support them. The coming-soon experience must make guarded visitors feel recognized without exposing, diagnosing, or speaking down to them, then provide a calm route to early access.

### Brand Personality

Protective, direct, and quietly defiant. Blue Corner should feel like an editorial public-interest campaign with the steadiness of someone waiting between rounds: human, plain-spoken, credible, and never performatively clinical.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the supplied reference as the one canonical visual system: off-white editorial pages, dominant bright Blue Corner blue, dark navy ink, rare yellow signal bars, hard rules, oversized left-anchored typography, numbered evidence, asymmetric compositions, and intact approved corner assets. The page uses a clean 3:2 empty-ring photograph with a DOM-rendered blue corner glyph; no interface text or controls are baked into raster art. Boxing appears through the metaphor of the corner, rope geometry, bell rhythm, canvas, tape, and held space—not fighters, sports-poster drama, games, glossy stock scenes, decorative gradients, or logo abuse.

### Design Principles

- Build one canonical editorial landing page plus a plain privacy page; do not reintroduce alternate routes, gallery framing, or add-on modules.
- Preserve the reference page’s section rhythm, density, hierarchy, and sharp geometry.
- Run full bleed: every section spans the viewport inside the 56px gutter, with line length held by per-element measures rather than a page-width cap. Keep the 46/48 hero, the four-column rooms grid, the 12.6% manifesto rail, the four-card plan, and the top-aligned 42/53 conversion split with a 720px maximum card. Display type scales with the viewport rather than capping at 1440px proportions.
- Five screens, in order: hero, the numbers, four rooms, the corner and its plan on one screen, sign-up. Each fills exactly one viewport and closes on the navy crisis band.
- Keep the locked line breaks in every core display heading; exactly one highlighted word per screen that carries a kicker chip. Purposeful on-entry reveals are allowed when they fail open and respect reduced motion.
- Use bright blue as the primary field; reserve navy for ink, the manifesto rail, the statistics band, the room captions and the crisis band.
- Keep yellow rare and functional: kicker chips, one highlighted word, statistics figures, room headings, active states, primary action emphasis, and the crisis tap targets.
- The crisis numbers are the most important thing on the page. They repeat on every screen as 44px tap targets with the helpline named, never as a row of underlines and never behind a decorative element.
- Photography carries the rooms: rooms with the man absent, one consistent grade, no fighters and no stock drama. Until the four shots are delivered, each slot renders its own commissioning brief.
- Make every claim sourceable, every form state explicit, every control keyboard-accessible, and every layout stable from 320px through desktop.
