What we do with the details you leave

Privacy

One form, one purpose: to email you when we open. Nothing else, and nobody else.

What the form collects

Whether you are joining as a patient or a therapist, your name, your email address, your phone number, and your tick confirming we may email you. The form only sends when you tick that box, and it is never ticked for you. Nothing is collected anywhere else on this site.

Where it goes

Straight to The Blue Corner team as a single email, and nowhere else. It is not added to a mailing list, an advertising audience, or a customer database, and it is never sold or shared. That email, and the confirmation we send you, are delivered by Resend, our email provider, which processes them on servers in the United States. If delivery to us fails, your details are held in a private file on our server so your signup is not lost, and nothing else is kept.

Analytics and cookies

There are none. No analytics, no tag manager, no advertising pixels, no cookies, and no local or session storage. We do not track you across this site or any other.

What loads from elsewhere

One stylesheet and its font files load from Adobe Typekit at use.typekit.net, which is how the licensed brand typeface is served. Everything else — images, styles, and scripts — is served from this site.

What you'll receive

An email straight away confirming we have your details, then someone from The Blue Corner getting in touch personally to put you on the list, and a note on the day we open. No newsletter, and nothing from anybody else.

Changing your mind

Reply to either email and we will take you off the list and delete your details, with no questions and no delay. You can do that at any time, including before we launch.

When we launch

The live Blue Corner will collect and protect personal health information under Canadian privacy law, and it will publish its own fuller policy before anyone can book care. This page covers the coming-soon page and its one form.
